From me to you

Dear Manuuuuuuuuu,

Happiest Birthday to one of the most special people I've met.

Honestly, when we met in 1st year, 1st sem, I never imagined that you would become this important to me.

Years passed so quickly. Sometimes it genuinely feels like we just met... and then I realise how many memories we've already collected.

We've laughed, irritated each other, made random memories, survived so many things and somehow, through all of it... we're still here.

So many people came into our lives and eventually went their own ways. But somehow, manam aithe inka unnam. And honestly, I think that's what makes our bond so special.

Mana iddaram pichollam... neku ekkuva pichi anuko, ayina kuda ninnu nen baristha. Nannu nuvvu baristhav. Endukante mana iddaram iddariki ekkuve.

Enni fights aina, enni irritations aina, okkokkasari okariki okaram chiraku teppinchina... last ki malli maname kalisi untam. Adhe mana bond.

Enthaina, naaku nuvvu thappa inka evarunnaru rawww.

And maybe that's what makes us special — we don't have to be perfect with each other. We can just be ourselves, completely crazy and completely comfortable.

You have always been there. You're loyal, you're crazy, you're sweet in your own way, and honestly... you're someone I never want to lose.

Nuvvu na life lo vachina oka normal person kaadhu.

You became one of those people whose presence just feels right.

So on your birthday, I don't just wish you success or happiness.

I wish that you always have reasons to smile. I wish you find people who genuinely value you. I wish you achieve everything you've dreamed about.

And whenever life gets difficult, I hope you remember that you've got someone cheering for you.

I hope life gives you everything you deserve. Happiness. Peace. Success. And countless moments that make you genuinely happy.

And no matter how much life changes, I hope this bond never becomes just another memory.

So please... ilaane undu.

Stay crazy.
Stay sweet.
Stay annoying.
And most importantly...

stay my special one.

Happiest Birthday, Manuuuuuuuuu.

Here's to all the memories we've already made... and all the ones still waiting for us.

With lots of love,
Your Special Someone
